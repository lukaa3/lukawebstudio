Frequently asked questions

Straight answers about ownership, pricing, and what happens after launch.

Do I own my website and domain name?

Yes! We guide you through purchasing your domain name directly on Namecheap so it remains in your name with 100% legal ownership. Once final payment is made, you own all site assets.

Is the demo mockup really free?

Yes. We design a custom homepage concept for your business at no cost and with no commitment. You only pay if you decide to hire us.

How does payment work?

You pay 50% to get started and the remaining 50% when your site is finished and ready to go live. The $49/mo Care Plan starts 30 days after launch.

What if I want changes after my site launches?

Minor revisions are included for 30 days after launch. After that, the $49/mo Care Plan covers ongoing text, phone number, and photo updates.
